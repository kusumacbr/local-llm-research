Acme Robotics - Expense Reimbursement Policy
============================================

Document ID: POL-FIN-012  
Effective Date: March 1, 2026  
Owner: Finance & Accounts (`finance@acmerobotics.internal`)  
Approval Authority: Director of Finance

---

1. General Guidelines
---------------------
- All expense reimbursement claims must be submitted via Zoho Expense within 30 calendar days of the transaction date.
- Receipts and itemized tax invoices are required for every claim above ₹200. Hand-written cash receipts are not accepted without prior written manager sign-off.

2. Approval Limits & Hierarchy
------------------------------
- Claims up to ₹5,000: Require only direct reporting manager approval.
- Expense claims above ₹5,000: Need director approval before processing by Finance.
- Claims above ₹50,000: Require approval from the Chief Executive Officer (Rajesh Sen).

3. Meal Allowances & Daily Per Diem
-----------------------------------
- During approved domestic business travel, meal expenses are reimbursed up to a flat cap of ₹1,200 per day upon submission of food bills. Alcohol is strictly non-reimbursable.
- Team dinners and morale events must be pre-approved by the department head and budget owner.

4. Hardware & Home Office Stipend
---------------------------------
- Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office setup reimbursement of up to ₹25,000 for ergonomic chairs, desks, or external monitors.
- Peripherals like cables and keyboards under ₹2,000 can be claimed through standard petty cash.

5. Settlement Timelines
-----------------------
- Approved expense reports are processed twice a month: on the 10th and 25th calendar days.
