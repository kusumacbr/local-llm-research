3. First-Week Checklist
-----------------------
1. Receive laptop and YubiKey from IT Support on Day 1.
2. Complete 2FA enrollment on Google Workspace and GitHub Enterprise.
3. Join primary Slack channels: `general`, `engineering-all`, `announcements`, `random`.
4. Review and accept the Information Security Policy (`SEC-POL-002`).
5. Meet your assigned onboarding buddy for a 1:1 coffee chat.

4. Probation Period
-------------------
- All regular full-time appointments are subject to a 3-month probation period.
- A formal performance review with your direct manager takes place at the end of Month 3 to confirm full employment status.

5. Working Hours & In-Office Schedule
-------------------------------------
- Standard working hours: 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM IST.
- Office Attendance: For all Bengaluru-based engineering personnel, mandatory in-office collaboration days are Mondays and Wednesdays. (Note: Remote arrangements for other days must be logged on the HRMS portal).
