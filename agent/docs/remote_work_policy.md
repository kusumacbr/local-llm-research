Acme Robotics - Remote & Hybrid Work Policy
===========================================

Document ID: POL-OPS-007  
Effective Date: February 15, 2026  
Owner: People Operations (`people-ops@acmerobotics.internal`)  

---

1. Hybrid Work Framework
------------------------
Acme Robotics operates on a hybrid office model to encourage cross-functional collaboration while maintaining flexibility:
- Core In-Office Days: Engineering and Product teams are required to work from the Bengaluru headquarters on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
- On Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays, team members may work remotely or from the office at their discretion.

2. Core Collaborative Hours
---------------------------
- To facilitate synchronous communication across distributed team members, all staff must be available on Slack and Google Meet during core hours:
  - 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST (Monday through Friday).

3. Remote Work Allowances
-------------------------
- Home Internet Stipend: Remote and hybrid employees are eligible for an internet connectivity reimbursement of up to ₹1,500 per month. Monthly invoices must be submitted under the "Telecom Expense" head in Zoho Expense.
- Mobile data boosters during travel can be claimed up to ₹500/month with manager approval.

4. Fully Remote Requests
------------------------
- Transitioning to a 100% remote arrangement requires written endorsement from your department director and final approval from Dr. Vikram Malhotra (VP of Engineering).

5. Working from Outside Base Country
------------------------------------
- Employees are not permitted to work outside India without advance clearance from Legal and Information Security due to data export regulations and taxation treaties.
