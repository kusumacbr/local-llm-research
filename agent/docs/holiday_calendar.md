Acme Robotics - Holiday Calendar 2026
=====================================

Document ID: HR-CAL-2026  
Applicable Location: Bengaluru HQ, India  
Published By: People Operations  

---

1. Mandatory Public Holidays (10 Days)
--------------------------------------
The company observes 10 paid public holidays in calendar year 2026:

| Date | Day | Holiday |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| January 26, 2026 | Monday | Republic Day |
| March 19, 2026 | Thursday | Ugadi |
| May 1, 2026 | Friday | May Day / Labour Day |
| August 15, 2026 | Saturday | Independence Day |
| October 2, 2026 | Friday | Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti |
| October 20, 2026 | Tuesday | Dussehra (Vijayadashami) |
| November 8, 2026 | Sunday | Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) |
| November 9, 2026 | Monday | Diwali (Balipratipada) |
| November 1, 2026 | Sunday | Kannada Rajyotsava |
| December 25, 2026 | Friday | Christmas Day |

2. Optional / Floating Holidays
-------------------------------
- Employees are entitled to choose 2 optional holidays per year from the following list:
  - Maha Shivaratri (February 15, 2026)
  - Good Friday (April 3, 2026)
  - Eid-ul-Fitr (March 21, 2026)
  - Ganesh Chaturthi (September 14, 2026)
  - Guru Nanak Jayanti (November 24, 2026)
- Optional holidays must be submitted through HRMS at least 5 business days in advance.
