Acme Robotics - Corporate Travel Policy
=======================================

Document ID: POL-TRV-004  
Effective Date: January 15, 2026  
Owner: Finance & Travel Desk (`traveldesk@acmerobotics.internal`)  

---

1. Scope & Booking Channels
---------------------------
- This policy applies to all business travel undertaken by employees of Acme Robotics.
- All domestic and international flight, train, and hotel reservations must be booked through the company's approved corporate travel portal (MakeMyTrip MyBiz) or via email to `traveldesk@acmerobotics.internal`.
- Self-booked airfare without prior Travel Desk sign-off will not be eligible for reimbursement.

2. Air & Rail Travel
--------------------
- Air Travel: Economy class is mandatory for all domestic flights and international flights under 8 hours. Flights must be booked at least 14 days prior to travel whenever practical.
- Rail Travel: 2-Tier AC or Executive Chair Car is permitted for intercity rail journeys under 6 hours.

3. Lodging & Hotel Caps
-----------------------
Maximum allowable room rates (excluding taxes) by city category:
- Tier 1 Cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai): Up to ₹4,500 per night.
- Tier 2 Cities (Pune, Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Chandigarh): Up to ₹3,200 per night.
- Other Locations: Up to ₹2,500 per night.

4. Daily Meals & Incidentals
----------------------------
- Domestic business travel meal per diem is capped at ₹1,200 per day (as detailed in `POL-FIN-012`). Itemized receipts must accompany expense reports.
- International Travel Per Diem: International daily per diem allowances are currently under formal review by the Finance Committee for FY27. All overseas expenses are temporarily approved on an ad-hoc, case-by-case basis via written email from `finance@acmerobotics.internal`.

5. Local Commutes During Travel
-------------------------------
- App-based cabs (Uber, Ola) for travel between airports, hotels, and client sites are fully reimbursable upon submitting digital invoices.
