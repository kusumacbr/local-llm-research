Acme Robotics - Leave Policy
============================

Document ID: POL-HR-003  
Effective Date: January 1, 2026  
Owner: Human Resources (`hr@acmerobotics.internal`)  
Target Audience: All full-time employees

---

1. Annual Leave (Privilege Leave)
---------------------------------
- Full-time employees accrue 18 days of annual leave per calendar year (1.5 days accrued per month).
- A maximum of 8 unused annual leave days can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any additional unused days beyond 8 will lapse on December 31.
- Leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive days must be submitted via the HRMS portal at least 7 business days in advance and require approval from your direct reporting manager.

2. Sick & Casual Leave
----------------------
- Casual Leave (CL): 6 days per calendar year. Intended for urgent personal matters. Maximum 2 consecutive days per instance.
- Sick Leave (SL): 10 days per calendar year. A registered medical practitioner's certificate is mandatory for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days.

3. Parental Leave
-----------------
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of paid leave for female employees, available for up to two surviving children.
- Paternity Leave: 15 calendar days of paid leave, which must be availed within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.

4. Half-Day Leaves
------------------
- Half-day leave slots follow standard core hours:
  - First Half: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM
  - Second Half: 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM

5. Unplanned Absence
--------------------
- Employees must notify their team lead and email `attendance@acmerobotics.internal` by 9:30 AM IST on the day of absence.
