## Task 1: What is the leave policy for new employees?

The leave policy for new employees at Acme Robotics is as follows:

* New employees are entitled to 20 days of paid vacation time upon hire, which can be used at the discretion of their supervisor.
* All employees are expected to take at least one paid vacation day per year, unless otherwise approved by their supervisor.
* Employees are also entitled to 10 paid sick leave days per year, which can be used for illness or injury.
* Employees are expected to use their paid time off in accordance with company policy and to provide at least two weeks' notice before taking a vacation or sick leave.
* Remote workers are entitled to the same leave policies as on-site employees, with the exception that remote workers are not entitled to paid vacation time.

Please note that these policies are subject to change, and employees should consult with their supervisor or HR representative for the most up-to-date information.
