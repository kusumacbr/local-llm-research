## Task 1: What is the leave policy for new employees?

The leave policy for new employees at Acme Robotics is as follows:

- **Annual Leave (Privilege Leave):** Full-time employees accrue **18 days** of annual leave per calendar year (1.5 days accrued per month). A maximum of **8 unused annual leave days** can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any additional unused days beyond 8 will lapse on December 31.
- **Sick & Casual Leave:** Casual leave (CL) is 6 days per calendar year. A maximum of 2 consecutive days per instance. Leave requests exceeding **3 consecutive days** must be submitted via the HRMS portal at least **7 business days in advance** and require approval from your direct reporting manager.
- **Parental Leave:** Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for female employees, available for up to two surviving children. Paternity leave is 15 calendar days of paid leave, which must be availed within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.
- **Half-Day Leaves:** Half-day leave slots follow standard core hours:
  - **First Half:** 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM
  - **Second Half:** 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM
- **Unplanned Absence:** Employees must notify their team lead and email `attendance@acmerobotics.internal` by **9:30 AM IST** on the day of absence.

## Task 2: Who approves expense claims above ₹5,000, and within how many days must claims be submitted?

According to the Acme Robotics' expense reimbursement policy, claims for expenses above ₹5,000 must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the transaction date. Claims for expenses up to ₹5,000 require only direct reporting manager approval, while claims above ₹50,000 require approval from the Chief Executive Officer. Claims above ₹50,000 require approval from the Director of Finance.
