Task 1: What is the leave policy for new employees?
---------------------------------------------------

Based on Acme Robotics' company documentation, full-time employees accrue 18 days of annual leave per calendar year (1.5 days accrued per month), 6 days of casual leave per calendar year, and 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. 

However, the company documents retrieved do not specify a separate leave policy or restrictions specifically for new employees (such as during their 3-month probation period).

Task 2: Who approves expense claims above ₹5,000, and within how many days must claims be submitted?
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Based on Acme Robotics' Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-FIN-012):

- Approval: Expense claims above ₹5,000 need director approval before being processed by Finance.
- Submission Deadline: All expense reimbursement claims must be submitted via Zoho Expense within 30 calendar days of the transaction date.
