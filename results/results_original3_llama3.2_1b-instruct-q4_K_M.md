Task 1: What is the leave policy for new employees?
-----------------------------------------------------

The leave policy for new employees at Acme Robotics is as follows:

- Annual Leave (Privilege Leave): 18 days of annual leave per calendar year, with a maximum of 8 unused annual leave days that can be carried forward into the next calendar year.
- Sick & Casual Leave: 6 days of casual leave (CL) per calendar year, with a maximum of 2 consecutive days per instance, and a registered medical practitioner's certificate required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days.
- Parental Leave: 26 weeks of paid leave for female employees, available for up to two surviving children, and 15 calendar days of paid leave for male employees, which must be availed within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.
- Half-Day Leaves: Half-day leave slots follow standard core hours, with a maximum of 2 consecutive days per instance.
- Unplanned Absence: Employees must notify their team lead and email attendance@acmerobotics.internal by 9:30 AM IST on the day of absence.
