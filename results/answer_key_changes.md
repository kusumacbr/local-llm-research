1. Disagreement Adjudications (One Line per Disagreement)

Article 1: Asthma (art_01)
- J30.9 (Allergic rhinitis): CHANGE to T78.40 (Allergy, unspecified) - Page text states "obesity and allergies" as cofactors without documenting rhinitis; guideline I.B.1 requires coding documented allergy (T78.40) rather than unsubstantiated allergic rhinitis (J30.9).
- E66.9 (Obesity, unspecified): ADD (secondary) - Obesity is explicitly documented on the source page under "Who is at risk for asthma?" as a major comorbid condition.

Article 2: High Blood Pressure (art_02)
- I51.9 (Heart disease, unspecified): CHANGE (Remove and replace with specific conditions) - Tabular list treats I51.9 as an unspecified wastebasket code; page text explicitly names "heart attack" (I21.9) and "heart failure" (I50.9), which guidelines mandate coding with specific encounter codes.
- I50.9 (Heart failure, unspecified): ADD (secondary) - Explicitly stated primary cardiovascular complication on source page ("leading to serious health problems such as heart attack, stroke, heart failure").
- I21.9 (Acute myocardial infarction, unspecified): ADD (secondary) - Explicitly stated cardiovascular complication ("heart attack") on source page.
- N19 (Kidney failure, unspecified): ADD (secondary) - Explicitly named target organ complication on source page ("heart attack, stroke, heart failure, and kidney failure").
- I63.9 (Stroke): CHANGE tier from tertiary to secondary - Page groups stroke with equal clinical prominence alongside heart attack, heart failure, and kidney failure.

Article 3: Pneumonia (art_03)
- U07.1 (COVID-19): ADD (secondary) - Explicitly named viral pathogen on source page under viral causes of pneumonia; Section I.C.1.g requires assigning U07.1 for COVID-19.
- A48.1 (Legionnaires' disease): ADD (secondary) - Explicitly named bacterial etiology on source page ("Legionella pneumophila; this type of pneumonia is often called Legionnaires' disease").
- J96.90 (Respiratory failure) and J85.2 (Lung abscess): KEEP OUT of answer key - Mentioned as acute ICU/inpatient sequelae rather than distinct outpatient diagnosis entities for a primary pneumonia test set.

Article 4: Alzheimer's Disease (art_04)
- F03.90 (Unspecified dementia): CHANGE to F02.80 (Dementia in other diseases classified elsewhere without behavioral disturbance) - Tabular List instructional note under G30 explicitly mandates: "Use additional code to identify: dementia (F02.8-)", and F03 contains an Excludes1 note prohibiting assignment with Alzheimer's disease (G30).
- G31.84 (Mild cognitive impairment): ADD (secondary) - Article features a dedicated clinical subsection defining mild cognitive impairment as the earliest symptomatic precursor.
- I10 (Hypertension) and H91.90 (Hearing loss): KEEP OUT of answer key - General lifestyle/cardiovascular risk factors listed in passing; not core Alzheimer's clinical entities.

Article 5: What is Chronic Kidney Disease? (art_05)
- D64.9 (Anemia, unspecified): ADD (tertiary) - Article explicitly details anemia as a direct hematologic consequence and complication of CKD.
- N17.9 (Acute kidney injury): KEEP OUT of answer key - Article explicitly contrasts CKD with sudden acute injury, focusing on chronic progressive failure.

Article 6: Chronic Kidney Disease and Diabetes (art_06)
- E11.22 (Type 2 diabetes with diabetic chronic kidney disease): KEEP (primary) - Guideline Section I.B.9 mandates assigning combination code E11.22 when both diabetes and CKD are present, rather than unbundling into separate codes.
- N18.9 (Chronic kidney disease, unspecified): KEEP (primary) - Tabular List instruction under E11.22 explicitly requires: "Use additional code to identify stage of chronic kidney disease (N18.1-N18.6)"; when stage is unspecified, N18.9 is the mandatory companion code.
- I63.9 (Stroke): ADD (tertiary) - Text explicitly notes that patients with diabetes and CKD face elevated risk for stroke alongside heart disease.
- I51.9 (Heart disease, unspecified): KEEP (tertiary) - Mentioned as a major comorbid vascular risk.

Article 7: Metabolic Syndrome (art_07)
- I25.10 (Atherosclerotic heart disease): CHANGE to I51.9 (Heart disease, unspecified) - Page text uses general phrase "heart disease" rather than specifying coronary atherosclerosis; I51.9 matches the clinical documentation.
- E78.5 (Hyperlipidemia, unspecified): KEEP (allow E78.2 Mixed hyperlipidemia) - Tabular note classifies mixed elevation of triglycerides and low HDL under E78.2, while general hyperlipidemia is E78.5; both represent valid clinical representations.

Article 8: Stroke (art_08)
- I48.91 (Atrial fibrillation): KEEP (tertiary) - Documented specifically as a primary cardiac source for embolic strokes.
- I61.9 (Nontraumatic intracerebral hemorrhage / Hemorrhagic stroke): ADD (primary) - Article explicitly defines hemorrhagic stroke with equal prominence to ischemic stroke as one of the two fundamental types of stroke.

Article 9: Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (art_09)
- J45.909 (Asthma): CHANGE (Remove from answer key) - On the restored real page text from NHLBI, asthma is not documented in the core COPD definition section (it was only in the rewritten summary).
- E88.01 (Alpha-1-antitrypsin deficiency): ADD (secondary) - Explicitly named genetic etiology of COPD in the restored NHLBI text ("A genetic condition called alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency can also cause the disease").

Article 10: Heart Failure (art_10)
- I42.9 (Cardiomyopathy, unspecified): ADD (secondary) - Explicitly documented under "What causes heart failure?" on the source page.
- I49.9 (Cardiac arrhythmia, unspecified): ADD (secondary) - Explicitly documented under "What causes heart failure?" on the source page.
